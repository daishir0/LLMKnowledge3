# Writing Quality and Communication Score (Numeric Only)

Assess the quality of writing and communication on a scale of 1-100 based on:
- Clarity and coherence of expression (25 points)
- Organization and structure of arguments (25 points)
- Grammar, mechanics, and style (20 points)
- Appropriate academic tone and voice (15 points)
- Effectiveness of introduction and conclusion (15 points)

ESSENTIAL: Return ONLY the numerical score between 1-100. No text, explanations, or additional characters.

Acceptable formats: 73, 88, 65
Unacceptable formats: "Writing score: 73", "73%", "Quality = 73"
