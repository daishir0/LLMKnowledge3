# Content Knowledge and Understanding Score (Numeric Only)

Rate the student's demonstration of content knowledge and understanding on a scale of 1-100 based on:
- Accuracy of factual information and concepts (25 points)
- Depth of understanding of key theories and principles (25 points)
- Appropriate use of discipline-specific terminology (20 points)
- Integration of course concepts and materials (20 points)  
- Evidence of reading and research beyond basic requirements (10 points)

CRITICAL: Output ONLY a single number from 1-100. No explanations, text, symbols, or additional content permitted.

Correct format examples: 78, 91, 67
Incorrect format examples: "Score: 78", "78/100", "The student demonstrates 78"
