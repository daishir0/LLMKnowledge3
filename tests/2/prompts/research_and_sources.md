# Research Quality and Source Usage Evaluation

Conduct a comprehensive assessment of the student's research methodology and source utilization:

## Source Quality and Appropriateness
- Evaluate the credibility and relevance of sources cited
- Assess the diversity and breadth of research materials used
- Analyze whether sources are current and appropriate for the discipline
- Review the balance between primary and secondary sources

## Integration and Citation
- Examine how well sources are integrated into the argument
- Assess the accuracy and consistency of citation format
- Evaluate whether sources are used to support claims effectively
- Check for appropriate attribution and avoidance of plagiarism

## Research Depth and Scope
- Analyze the thoroughness of literature review and background research
- Evaluate whether research addresses multiple perspectives on the topic
- Assess if the student has engaged with opposing viewpoints
- Review the scope of research relative to assignment requirements

## Evidence and Support
- Examine the quality of evidence presented to support arguments
- Assess whether claims are adequately substantiated
- Evaluate the logical connection between evidence and conclusions
- Review the use of examples, data, and expert testimony

Provide specific feedback on strengths and areas for improvement in research practices.
