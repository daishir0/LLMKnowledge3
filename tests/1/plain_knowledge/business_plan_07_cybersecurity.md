## Executive Summary

**Company**: SecureGuard Solutions  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $2.7M Seed Round

SecureGuard provides enterprise-grade cybersecurity protection designed specifically for small and medium businesses. Our AI-powered platform delivers 24/7 threat detection, automated response, and compliance management at a fraction of traditional enterprise security costs.

## Market Problem

**SMB Cybersecurity Gap**:
- 43% of cyberattacks target small businesses
- 60% of SMBs go out of business within 6 months of a cyberattack
- Average cost of data breach for SMBs: $2.98M
- 95% of SMBs lack dedicated security personnel

**Current Solutions Inadequate**:
- Enterprise solutions too expensive ($50K-$500K annually)
- Basic antivirus insufficient for modern threats
- Lack of 24/7 monitoring and response
