# SkillBridge: Corporate Training Platform with AI Personalization

## Executive Summary

**Company**: SkillBridge Learning  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $2.0M Seed Round  
**Valuation**: $10M pre-money  

SkillBridge transforms corporate training through AI-powered personalized learning paths. Our platform adapts content delivery based on individual learning styles, job roles, and performance data to maximize training ROI.

## Market Overview

**Corporate Training Market**: $366B globally, $169B in North America
**Digital Learning Segment**: $43B, growing at 15% CAGR
**Key Trends**: Remote work adoption, skills gap widening, personalization demand

**Pain Points**:
- 70% of corporate training is forgotten within 24 hours
- One-size-fits-all approach ineffective
- Difficult to measure training ROI
- High cost of live instructor-led training

## Product Description

**Core Platform**:
- AI-driven learning path recommendations
- Microlearning modules (5-15 minutes)
- Skills assessment and gap analysis
- Social learning and peer collaboration
- Real-time progress tracking and analytics

**Content Library**:
- 2,000+ courses across 15 skill categories
- Custom content creation tools
- Industry-specific certification paths
- Multi-format delivery (video, interactive, VR)

**AI Features**:
- Learning style identification
- Adaptive content difficulty
- Optimal timing recommendations
- Performance prediction models

## Technology Stack

**Platform Architecture**:
- Microservices on AWS/Docker
- React frontend, Node.js backend
- Machine learning pipeline (Python/TensorFlow)
- SCORM/xAPI compliance
- Mobile-responsive design

**AI/ML Capabilities**:
- Natural language processing for content analysis
- Collaborative filtering for recommendations
- Computer vision for engagement tracking
- Predictive analytics for learning outcomes
