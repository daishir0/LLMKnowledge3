# RentSmart: AI-Powered Property Management Platform

## Executive Summary

**Company**: RentSmart Technologies  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $4.5M Series A  
**Valuation**: $22M pre-money  

RentSmart revolutionizes property management through AI-driven automation, predictive maintenance, and tenant experience optimization. Our platform serves property managers, landlords, and tenants with integrated solutions that reduce operational costs by 35%.
