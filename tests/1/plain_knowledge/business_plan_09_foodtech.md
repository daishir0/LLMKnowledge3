# FreshTrack: AI-Powered Food Supply Chain Optimization

## Executive Summary

**Company**: FreshTrack Solutions  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $3.5M Series A  
**Valuation**: $16M pre-money  

FreshTrack revolutionizes food supply chain management through AI-powered demand forecasting, inventory optimization, and quality monitoring. Our platform reduces food waste by 40% while improving profit margins for restaurants, grocery stores, and food distributors.

## Market Problem

**Food Waste Crisis**:
- $1.3 trillion in food wasted globally annually
- 30-40% of US food supply is wasted
- Restaurants waste $162B worth of food yearly
- Grocery stores lose 10% of inventory to spoilage

**Supply Chain Inefficiencies**:
- Poor demand forecasting leads to overordering
- Manual inventory management systems
- Lack of real-time quality monitoring
- Limited visibility across supply chain tiers

## Solution Overview

**Core Platform Features**:
- AI-driven demand forecasting and inventory optimization
- Real-time food quality monitoring using IoT sensors
- Supply chain visibility and traceability platform
- Automated ordering and replenishment systems
- Food safety compliance and documentation

**Smart Sensors & IoT**:
- Temperature and humidity monitoring

- Smart scales for inventory tracking
