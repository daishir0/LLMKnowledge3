# FinanceFlow: SMB Cash Flow Management Platform

## Executive Summary

**Company**: FinanceFlow Inc.  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $1.8M Seed Round  
**Valuation**: $8M pre-money  

FinanceFlow provides automated cash flow forecasting and management tools for small and medium businesses. Our platform integrates with existing accounting software to provide real-time insights and predictive analytics.

## Market Analysis

The SMB financial management software market is $4.2B globally, growing at 8.5% annually. Pain points include:
- 82% of SMBs fail due to cash flow problems
- Manual forecasting takes 15-20 hours monthly
- Limited visibility into future cash positions

Target market: 28M SMBs in North America with $1M-$50M annual revenue.

## Product Overview

**Key Features**:
- Automated cash flow forecasting (13-week rolling)
- Bank account aggregation and categorization
- Invoice tracking and payment predictions
- Scenario planning and stress testing
- Mobile app for business owners

**Integrations**:
- QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks
- Major banks via Plaid/Yodlee
- Payment processors (Stripe, Square)
- CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot)

## Technology
