# FleetOptima: AI-Driven Last-Mile Delivery Optimization

## Executive Summary

**Company**: FleetOptima Inc.  
**Founded**: 2024  
**Funding Requested**: $3.8M Series A  
**Valuation**: $18M pre-money  

FleetOptima uses advanced AI algorithms to optimize last-mile delivery operations, reducing delivery costs by 25% and improving customer satisfaction through accurate ETAs and route optimization. Our platform serves e-commerce businesses, food delivery services, and logistics companies.

## Market Opportunity

**Last-Mile Delivery Market**: $130B globally, 22% CAGR
**Pain Points**:
- Last-mile represents 53% of total shipping costs
- Failed deliveries cost $17.2B annually
- Driver shortage affecting 65% of logistics companies
- Customer expectations for same-day delivery increasing

**Target Market**: 15,000+ businesses with delivery operations in North America
