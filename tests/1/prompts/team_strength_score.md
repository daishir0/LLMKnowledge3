# Team Strength Score (Numeric Only)

Rate the team strength and execution capability on a scale of 1-100 considering:
- Founder/CEO background and relevant experience (30 points)
- Technical team capabilities and track record (25 points)
- Advisory board quality and industry connections (20 points)
- Team completeness and key role coverage (15 points)
- Previous startup or scale experience (10 points)

MANDATORY: Return ONLY the numeric score. No words, punctuation, or explanations permitted.

Valid responses: 85, 42, 96
Invalid responses: "85 out of 100", "Team score = 85", "Good team: 85"
