# Business Model and Revenue Analysis

Conduct a thorough analysis of the business model, revenue streams, and commercial viability. Address the following areas:

## Revenue Model Evaluation
- Analyze the proposed pricing strategy and market positioning
- Evaluate revenue stream diversity and sustainability
- Assess customer acquisition and retention strategies
- Review unit economics and path to profitability

## Market Strategy Assessment
- Examine the go-to-market strategy and customer acquisition channels
- Evaluate competitive positioning and differentiation
- Analyze target customer segments and addressable market size
- Review partnership and distribution strategies

## Financial Projections Review
- Assess the realism and logic of financial forecasts
- Evaluate key assumptions underlying revenue and cost projections
- Analyze cash flow requirements and funding milestones
- Review sensitivity to key variables and market conditions

## Scalability and Growth Potential
- Evaluate the business model's ability to scale efficiently
- Analyze operational leverage and margin expansion opportunities
- Assess international expansion potential and market barriers
- Review long-term competitive sustainability

Provide recommendations for business model optimization and risk mitigation.
