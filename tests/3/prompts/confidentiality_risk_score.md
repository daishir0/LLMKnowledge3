# Confidentiality Risk Score (Numeric Only)

Evaluate the confidentiality risk level of this document on a scale of 1-100:
- TOP SECRET/Highly Confidential (trade secrets, classified info): 80-100 points
- CONFIDENTIAL (proprietary data, internal strategies): 60-79 points
- RESTRICTED (internal use, limited distribution): 40-59 points
- INTERNAL (company information, not public): 20-39 points
- PUBLIC (publicly available information): 1-19 points

CRITICAL: Output ONLY a single number from 1-100. No explanations, text, symbols, or additional content allowed.

Correct format: 75
Incorrect format: "Confidentiality: 75", "75%", "The confidentiality level is 75"
