# Marketing Campaign Strategy - Public Launch

## Campaign Overview
Public marketing campaign for our new product line targeted at millennials and Gen Z.

## Target Demographics
- Age: 22-35
- Income: $35K-$75K
- Urban and suburban markets
- Tech-savvy consumers

## Advertising Channels
- Social media (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- Google Ads and SEO
- Influencer partnerships
- Trade show presentations

## Budget Allocation
- Digital advertising: $2.5M
- Content creation: $800K
- Events and sponsorships: $1.2M

This document contains general marketing strategy information suitable for public sharing.
